# FitIn — Marketing Content

This folder contains ideas and scripts for social media marketing content.

## Content Types

### Instagram Reels / TikTok
- **Workout demos** — Short-form exercise form videos with overlay text
- **Before/after transformations** — Weight tracking journey clips
- **Meal prep montage** — Quick regional meal prep from the app

### Twitter / X
- **Daily tips** — Quick fitness and nutrition tips
- **Feature announcements** — New features, new meals, new exercises
- **Polls** — "What split do you follow?" engagement posts

### YouTube Shorts
- **App walkthrough** — 60-second app tour
- **Exercise tutorials** — Proper form guides
- **Meal comparisons** — India vs Germany vs USA meal plans
