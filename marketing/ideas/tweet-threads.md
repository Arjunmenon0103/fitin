## Thread 2: Progressive Overload Tips
1/ Progressive overload is the #1 key to building muscle.

But most people do it wrong.

Here are 5 ways to actually progress 🧵

2/ Add weight (the obvious one)
Even 1kg more is progress. Don't ego lift.

3/ Add reps
Went from 8 to 10 reps? That's overload.

4/ Add sets
An extra set per exercise adds volume over time.

5/ Slow down the tempo
3 seconds down, 1 second up. Your muscles don't know the weight, they know tension.

6/ Reduce rest time
Same weight, same reps, less rest = harder workout.
